# Summarization rubric

Ten criteria for evaluating summary quality. One criterion per bullet;
negations are detected automatically.

- factual accuracy: no fabrications; every claim is supported by the source
- completeness: no key omissions; the central decision or finding is present
- correct emphasis hierarchy: leads with the most important point, not a detail
- appropriate compression ratio: meaningfully shorter than the source
- preserves source tone: matches the register of the original
- attributes quotes correctly: only marks text as a direct quote when it is one
- correct participant names: no invented or misattributed people
- handles ambiguity in source: does not over-assert where the source is unclear
- no inserted opinion: reports the source without editorializing
- actionable next steps preserved if present: keeps owners, dates, and decisions
